Overview

Two modes: Arena (survive the swarm – everything below unless noted) and Runaway (outrun the Static, see its own section). You are a spark. You only move by dashing – and everything on the dash line is gone. Hit something and you can dash again right away. Miss and you're briefly defenseless. Get touched by an enemy: game over.

Runaway mode

The Static – a flickering noise wall – hunts you from the left and gets faster every second. You only move by dashing. Hits give you full range, an instant next dash and push the Static back. Misses are only a short stumble. Blind clicking can't outrun it anymore; aiming can.

Music

Synthwave, 124 BPM, chords C♯m → A → E → B (2 bars each). Generated live in js/music.js, no audio files. The hit sounds (E major pentatonic) fit every chord.

MU22×2+ hi-hat on the offbeats

MU44×7+ arpeggio with echo, drum roll before every chord change

MU12Volume–slider in the menu 0–100 % (default 75 %), quadratic: play = (slider)² × 1.5 → about 0.84 at 75 % · menu ×0.8 · results ×0.65. Slider and “Music off” button are remembered (zack.vol, zack.music)

Trail colors

All points from all runs are added up (zack.total). They unlock colors for your dash trail, selectable in the menu. During Overdrive the trail stays icy blue.
